About

I like robots that work outside the lab.

I'm a robotics and ML engineer focused on the messy gap between a model that works in simulation and a robot that works on a Tuesday afternoon. My work spans perception, planning, and control, with a soft spot for getting learned policies to survive contact with reality.

Recently I've been working on autonomous navigation and learned manipulation. Before that, embedded vision on edge hardware. I care about clean interfaces, reproducible experiments, and systems that fail gracefully.

Outside of work you'll find me tinkering with hardware, reading control theory I half understand, and over-documenting side projects.
